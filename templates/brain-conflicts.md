# <BRAIN-NAME> — conflicts

Any time two proofs disagree, record it here. **Never pick one silently.** The full process is
in `SKILL.md` → "Conflict process".

A conflict is not a failure of the brain — it is the brain working. Hiding one is the failure.

## Format

```
### C-nnn: <what disagrees, in one line>
- Facts involved: F-nnn, F-nnn
- Old claim (<date>): <what was recorded, and its proof>
- New claim (<date>): <what contradicts it, and its proof>
- Fake conflict check: <different zone? different time? different identity? — if yes, both are
  true and the Keys get split instead>
- What would settle it: `<the exact probe>`
- Status: OPEN — both facts badged ⚠️ UNVERIFIED (conflict C-nnn)
- Settled: <date> — <which won, why> | the loser's value moved to its History with
  "replaced by proof on <date>"
- Lesson: <if this conflict revealed a process gap, say what changes so it cannot recur>
```

The **Lesson** line matters. A conflict that teaches something should change a rule, not just
get closed.
