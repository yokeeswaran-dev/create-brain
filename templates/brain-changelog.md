# <BRAIN-NAME> — changelog

Every change to this brain, dated, **newest at the bottom**. Name who made it, so the next
person knows who to ask.

An entry says what changed and *why it matters* — not just which IDs moved. The one-line
capture summary (`+3 new, 2 updated, 1 conflict`) belongs here too.

## Format

```
- <date> (<who>) — <MODE>: <what changed> — <why it matters, if it is not obvious>
```
